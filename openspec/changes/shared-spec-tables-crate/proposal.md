## Why

The next decoder reconstruction brick is the AV2 § 7.15 inverse transform, which
needs the § 9.6/§ 9.7 transform-kernel tables. Those generated tables currently
live in `crates/splot-core/src/tables/`, but `splot-recon` may not depend on
`splot-core` (the one-way dependency rule). The maintainer chose to factor the
shared kernel tables into a tiny dependency-free crate that any crate may use.

The transform-kernel tables (`transform_1d`, `secondary_transform`) have no
consumer in `splot-core` today (they were generated ahead of need), so moving
them is zero-churn for `splot-core` apart from relocating one mirror cross-check
spot test.

## What Changes

- Add Feature ID `INFRA-SHARED-SPEC-TABLES`.
- Add a new dependency-free `splot-tables` crate (no `splot-*` and no external
  dependency) as the single home for the AV2 § 9 transform-kernel tables.
- Teach `cargo xtask gen-tables` a per-module output directory (`output_dir_for`):
  the § 9.6 `transform_1d` and § 9.7 `secondary_transform` modules are generated
  into `crates/splot-tables/src/tables/`; the other five § 9 modules stay in
  `crates/splot-core/src/tables/`. The generator writes and drift-checks both
  output directories.
- Relocate the `transform_1d` mirror cross-check spot test from `splot-core` to
  `splot-tables`.
- Register the crate in the workspace, `[workspace.dependencies]`, and the
  `check-dependency-direction` rules as a dependency-free leaf.
- Update the architecture docs and feature tracking.

Non-goals:

- No table content changes — the generated bytes are byte-identical (the
  determinism test's 236-table count is unchanged).
- No § 7.15 inverse-transform code, and no `splot-recon -> splot-tables`
  dependency yet; that consumer edge lands with the inverse-transform row.
- No move of the in-`splot-core` § 9 tables that have consumers there (`cdf`,
  `conversion`, `quantizer`, `warp_filter`, `loop_restoration`).
- No new external dependency.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `shared-spec-tables`: a dependency-free crate that holds the shared AV2 § 9
  transform-kernel tables generated by `cargo xtask gen-tables`.

### Modified Capabilities

None.
