## Why

The § 7.14.4 dequantization process (`RECON-DEQUANT-PROCESS`) takes a resolved
per-coefficient quantizer `q2`. When `using_qmatrix`, step 2 weights it by the
quantization matrix: `q2 = Round2(q * m, 5)`. Implementing that weighting for the
built-in `Quantizer_Matrix` is the next residual-path step, and it requires the
§ 9.4 quantizer-matrix tables to be reachable from `splot-recon`.

## What Changes

- Add Feature ID `RECON-DEQUANT-QM-WEIGHT`.
- Relocate the generated § 9.4 `quantizer` table module (`QUANTIZER_MATRIX`,
  `QM_OFFSET`) from `splot-core::tables` to the dependency-free `splot-tables`
  crate via the per-module `output_dir_for` routing in `cargo xtask gen-tables`
  (the same mechanism `INFRA-SHARED-SPEC-TABLES` used for the § 9.6/§ 9.7
  kernels). The module has no `splot-core` consumer, so the move is byte-identical
  and zero-churn (the 236-table determinism count is unchanged).
- Add `quantization_matrix_weight(QmWeightIndex)` (the built-in
  `Quantizer_Matrix[segLvl][plane > 0][Qm_Offset[txSz] + i*tw + j]` lookup, with
  bounds-checked indices) and `qm_weighted_quantizer(q, m)` (the § 7.14.4 step-2
  `Round2(q * m, 5)`, total in `i64` clamped into `u32`) to
  `crates/splot-recon/src/dequant_process.rs`. The caller feeds the weighted
  `q2` to `dequant_coefficient`.
- Add the typed `ReconError::InvalidQuantizerMatrixIndex`.
- Update documentation (the shared-tables enumeration in the implementation
  matrix, `AGENTS.md`, and `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` now list § 9.4 alongside
  § 9.6/§ 9.7), the roadmap, generated status docs, and OpenSpec artifacts.

Non-goals:

- No `useQm` / `useUserQm` / `segLvl` gating, no user-defined `UserQm` matrices,
  no `shift` / `useFsc` derivation, no coefficient entropy decode, and no
  inverse-transform invocation.
- No table-content change (the generated bytes are identical after the move).
- No tile-syntax decode, runtime decode output, hashes, Y4M, or reference
  refresh; no scheduler state in `splot-recon`.
- No AVM/dav2d source, dependency, wrapper, script, CI job, or required test.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `decoder-support`: records source-backed AV2 § 7.14.4 quantization-matrix
  weighting (the built-in `Quantizer_Matrix` path) and the § 9.4 table relocation
  to `splot-tables`, while broader reconstruction (the `useQm`/`UserQm` gating,
  the coefficient entropy decode, and the inverse-transform invocation) remains
  partial.
